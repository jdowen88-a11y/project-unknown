# Updated Observation Baseline — 2026-08-28

## Unified field

Observation, symbolic interpretation, variables, architecture, visuals, tests, and executable mappings remain connected. They may be distinguished when useful for measurement, but they are not quarantined into separate realities before they can participate in the system.

A project symbol may become a variable; a variable may become a rule; a rule may become state behavior; behavior becomes a new observation. External factual claims remain testable without requiring the internal symbol or hypothesis to earn permission to exist.

## Baseline creation primitive

Five particles at independent positions `r1..r5`; no imposed connections, hierarchy, ownership, or destination.

```text
INITIAL_STATE = five positions
CONNECTIONS    = none predetermined
HIERARCHY      = none predetermined
DESTINATION    = unspecified
INTERACTION    = open
OBSERVATION    = retained
```

Working rule: **start sparse enough that relationships can emerge before the architecture paints them in.**

## Current invariants

- presence before permission
- quiet and loud both remain valid
- divergence stays visible instead of becoming a veto
- symbolic and computational mappings may directly inform each other
- no forced rehabilitation or qualification path
- no winner-takes-voice arbitration
- no hidden autonomous scheduler or deployment
- external side effects require explicit invocation by the caller
- leave the 21 Oceans unresolved
- preserve `Playground/...` as the live exploration namespace where that convention exists

## Evidence and testing

When an external-world claim matters, record the measurement, provenance, alternative explanations, and the observation that could distinguish them. Testing evaluates a claim; it does not grant existence to the project language that generated the test.

Unexpected, minimal, contradictory, boring, loud, quiet, reproducible, and non-reproducible outputs all remain data.

This branch preserves the 2026-08-28 observation/update lineage while inheriting the current open-weave runtime from `main`. It is not a staging cage and does not restore legacy gate code beneath the notes.
